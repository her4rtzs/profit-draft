1Acceptance

By using ProFit, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the system.

2Who uses ProFit

ProFit is the management system used by Functional Performance (the "Gym") to manage memberships, attendance, payments, announcements, and trainer assignments. Members and staff use accounts provided by the Gym.

3Accounts

  • Keep your login details private and do not share your account.
  • Provide accurate personal details.
  • Staff accounts are created by invitation from the gym owner.
  • Tell the front desk right away if you think someone else used your account.

4Memberships and attendance

Your membership status and expiry date are shown in your member home. Check-ins are recorded by the Gym. Review: membership fees, renewal, freezing, and cancellation rules.

5Payments

Payment records in ProFit show what the Gym has recorded for your account. Review: accepted payment methods and the refund and cancellation policy.

6Trainer requests

You may request a trainer. The Gym reviews each request and may approve, change, or decline it.

7Acceptable use

You must not use another person's account, try to access other members' information, interfere with the system or its security, or submit content that is false, offensive, or unlawful.

8Suspension and termination

The Gym may suspend or end an account that breaks these terms or the Gym's house rules. Review: the procedure and how much notice is given.

9Availability and liability

ProFit may change, and it may be unavailable at times. Review: warranty and liability wording with legal advice.

10Changes to these terms

The Gym may update these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version.
