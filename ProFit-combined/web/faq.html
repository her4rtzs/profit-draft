Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about signing in, membership, payments, attendance, and contacting the gym. For anything else, contact the gym.

How do I sign in?

Use the username or email and password given to you by the Gym. Staff use the account the owner created for them.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Online password reset is not available yet. Please ask the gym front desk to reset it for you.

How do I check my membership status?

After you sign in, your member home shows your membership status and expiry date.

Where can I see my payments?

Your recent paid invoices are listed on your member home.

How do I check my attendance?

Your recent gym check-ins are listed on your member home.

How do I request a trainer?

You can request a trainer. The Gym reviews every request and may approve, change, or decline it. If you cannot find the option, ask the front desk.

Where do gym announcements appear?

The latest notices from the gym appear under Latest from the gym on your member home.

How do I contact the gym?

Is my information safe?

The Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how it is stored, and who can access it.
