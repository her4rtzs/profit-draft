This policy explains what personal data ProFit collects, why, and how it is handled. The data controller is Functional Performance.

1Information we collect

  • Account: full name, username, email address, and password. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.
  • Profile: phone number, address, and profile photo, where provided.
  • Membership: plan or status, start and expiry dates, and check-in records.
  • Payments: amounts, dates, and payment references.
  • Health and goals: information entered in the health and goals form.
  • Trainers and notifications: assigned trainer, trainer requests, and notification read status.
  • Verification: one-time codes and staff invitation links. These are stored only as hashes and expire after a set time.

2Why we use it

To manage memberships and attendance, record payments, send account verification codes, staff invitations, and announcements, keep accounts secure, and keep the Gym's records.

3Who can see it

Gym staff see only what their role allows. Members see their own information.

4Service providers

Data is processed through Supabase (database hosting, in the ap-southeast-2 Sydney region), Vercel (application hosting), and Resend (email delivery).

5Security

ProFit is served over HTTPS in production. Passwords and one-time codes are stored as hashes, and access is limited by role. No system is completely secure, so please report any suspected problem to the Gym.

6Browser storage

Some staff screens may still keep data in your browser's local storage while the system is being updated.

8Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you may ask to be informed about how your data is used, access your data, correct inaccurate data, object to certain processing, request erasure or blocking where the law allows, and complain to the National Privacy Commission.
