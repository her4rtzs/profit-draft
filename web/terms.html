1Acceptance

By using ProFit, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the system.

2Who uses ProFit

ProFit is the management system used by Functional Performance (the "Gym") to manage memberships, attendance, payments, announcements, and trainer assignments. Members and staff use accounts provided by the Gym.

3Accounts

  • Keep your login details private and do not share your account.
  • Provide accurate personal details when registering or updating your profile.
  • Member accounts may be created through free self sign-up with a seven-day trial, or by authorized gym staff. Staff accounts are created only by invitation from the gym owner.
  • Tell the front desk right away if you think someone else used your account.

4Memberships and attendance

Your membership plan, status, and expiry date are shown in your member home. A free seven-day trial gives app access only, with no gym membership and no check-ins. Active membership is required for gym access, and check-ins are recorded by the Gym. Memberships automatically expire at the end of the selected plan period unless renewed.

5Payments

Sign-up is free and starts a seven-day trial with app access only. A gym membership is activated by a cash payment recorded at the desk, or by a GCash payment with a reference number and a screenshot link that gym staff confirm. Submitting a payment does not grant access. Old online payment records from the retired gateway remain as paymongo history and are never changed.

6Trainer requests

You may request a trainer. The Gym reviews each request and may approve, change, or decline it.

7Acceptable use

You must not use another person's account, try to access other members' information, interfere with the system or its security, or submit content that is false, offensive, or unlawful.

8Suspension and termination

The Gym may suspend or end an account that breaks these terms or the Gym's house rules.

9Availability and liability

ProFit is provided on an as-available basis and may be temporarily unavailable during maintenance or service updates.

10Changes to these terms

The Gym may update these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version.
