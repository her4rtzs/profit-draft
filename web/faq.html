Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about signing in, membership, payments, attendance, Terms of Service, and Privacy Policy. For anything else, contact the gym.

How do I sign in?

Use the email or username and password you set when joining online, or the account details provided by the Gym. Staff use the account created via the owner's invitation.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Use the Forgot password? link on the Sign in page. Enter your account email to receive a 6-digit verification code, then enter the code and set a new password. If you still need help, contact the gym front desk.

How do I check my membership status?

After you sign in, your member home shows your membership status and expiry date.

Where can I see my payments?

Your recent paid invoices are listed on your member home.

How do I check my attendance?

Your recent gym check-ins are listed on your member home.

How do I request a trainer?

You can request a trainer from your member home. The Gym reviews every request and may approve, change, or decline it. If you cannot find the option, ask the front desk.

Where do gym announcements appear?

The latest notices from the gym appear under Latest from the gym on your member home.

How do I contact the gym?

Call 0906 053 6064, email FunctionalPerformance.2022@gmail.com, or visit Luksuhin - Mangas Rd., Saray, Mangas 1, Alfonso, Cavite.

Is my information safe?

Yes. Passwords and verification codes are stored only as hashes, production traffic runs over HTTPS, and access is limited by role. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service tabs for full details.

The rules for using ProFit, the gym management system used by Functional Performance.

Last updated: October 2026

1Acceptance

By using ProFit, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the system.

2Who uses ProFit

ProFit is the management system used by Functional Performance (the "Gym") to manage memberships, attendance, payments, announcements, and trainer assignments. Members and staff use accounts provided by the Gym.

3Accounts

  • Keep your login details private and do not share your account.
  • Provide accurate personal details when registering or updating your profile.
  • Member accounts are created through free self sign-up with a seven-day trial, or by authorized gym staff. Staff accounts are created only by invitation from the gym owner.
  • Tell the front desk right away if you think someone else used your account.

4Memberships and attendance

Your membership plan, status, and expiry date are shown in your member home. Active membership is required for gym access, and check-ins are recorded by the Gym. Memberships automatically expire at the end of the selected plan period unless renewed.

5Payments

Sign-up is free and starts a seven-day trial with app access only. To activate a gym membership, pay cash at the desk or submit a GCash payment with a reference number and a screenshot link. Staff review the payment before your membership starts. Old online payment records from the retired gateway remain as paymongo history and are never changed.

6Trainer requests

You may request a trainer. The Gym reviews each request and may approve, change, or decline it.

7Acceptable use

You must not use another person's account, try to access other members' information, interfere with the system or its security, or submit content that is false, offensive, or unlawful.

8Suspension and termination

The Gym may suspend or end an account that breaks these terms or the Gym's house rules.

9Availability and liability

ProFit is provided on an as-available basis and may be temporarily unavailable during maintenance or service updates.

10Changes to these terms

What personal information ProFit handles, why, and how it is protected.

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what personal data ProFit collects, why, and how it is handled. The data controller is Functional Performance.

1Information we collect

  • Account: full name, username, email address, and password. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.
  • Profile: phone number, address, and profile photo, where provided.
  • Membership: plan or status, start and expiry dates, and check-in records.
  • Payments: amounts, dates, and payment references.
  • Health and goals: information entered in the health and goals form.
  • Trainers and notifications: assigned trainer, trainer requests, and notification read status.
  • Verification: one-time codes and staff invitation links. These are stored only as hashes and expire after a set time.

2Why we use it

To manage memberships and attendance, record payments, send account verification codes, staff invitations, and announcements, keep accounts secure, and keep the Gym's records.

3Who can see it

Gym staff see only what their role allows. Members see their own information.

4Service providers

Data is processed through Supabase (database hosting in the ap-southeast-2 Sydney region and file storage), Vercel (application hosting), and Resend (transactional email delivery). The former online payment gateway is no longer used; only its historical records remain in ProFit.

5Security

ProFit is served over HTTPS in production. Passwords and one-time codes are stored as hashes, and access is limited by role. No system is completely secure, so please report any suspected problem to the Gym.

6Browser storage

Some staff screens may still keep data in your browser's local storage while the system is being updated.

7How long we keep it

We keep your account, profile, membership, attendance, payment, and trainer records for as long as your account remains with the Gym and as needed for gym operations, payment reconciliation, and record-keeping. When a membership expires, your status changes to expired while your attendance and payment history remain stored so you can renew without losing your records. Email verification codes expire after 10 minutes and are single-use, and staff invitation links expire after 72 hours. You may ask the Gym to correct, block, or erase personal data where the Data Privacy Act of 2012 allows, subject to applicable accounting and legal retention requirements.

8Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you may ask to be informed about how your data is used, access your data, correct inaccurate data, object to certain processing, request erasure or blocking where the law allows, and complain to the National Privacy Commission.
